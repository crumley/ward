## Purpose

What Ward is, the problem it solves, and the boundaries of the effort — the _why_ that every other
slice serves.

## Planned sections

- **What Ward is** — a CLI + toolset that operates opinionated, structured, self-sufficient
  workspaces where humans and agents work together on software.
- **The problem: attention/context is the scarce resource** — on both the agent side (finite window)
  and the human side (paged-out state after interruption).
- **The prime directive: context management** — the lens for resolving unclear design choices. Its
  dimensions: placement, focus, specialization, economy.
- **The setting** — local-and-personal workspace working on shared, remote repositories; the
  local↔remote boundary as a first-class concern.
- **Pause and resume as a baseline assumption.**
- **The metaphor: a hospital ward** — and the everyday vocabulary it buys.
- **A workspace that compounds** — on a cadence and at scope boundaries.
- **Weight** — scales down to one-off work as gracefully as up to long-lived efforts.
- **Non-goals.**

## Canonical home for

The prime directive (context management) and the hospital-ward metaphor. Principles elaborate them;
concepts apply them; everything links here for the _why_.
